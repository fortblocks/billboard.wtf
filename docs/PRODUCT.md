# billboard.wtf — Product Definition

**Working title:** billboard.wtf  
**Edition:** 2026  
**Domain:** billboard.wtf (confirmed)

## Vision

billboard.wtf is a permanent, zoomable, game-like pixel billboard that serves as a creative directory and backlink platform for vibecoders and builders.

It turns the simple act of claiming digital space into a shareable, status-bearing, story-rich experience. Each plot is a permanent public record of someone who was building in 2026.

**Core value proposition:**
- Permanent backlink on a living internet artifact
- Visual ownership + storytelling (Builder Card)
- Status and “I was early” signal
- Discoverability among other builders
- Fun, game-like interactions that make claiming space feel special

## Product Principles

1. **The grid is the product** — the page loads directly into the live board.
2. **Fun first, economics second** — keep systems simple enough that delight stays primary.
3. **Exemplary execution** — because the product is not overly complex, design and interaction quality must be exceptional.
4. **Permanent by default** — most space is owned forever once claimed.
5. **Light competition** — one special dynamic zone (The Crown) adds ongoing energy.
6. **Annual editions** — architecture supports billboard.wtf/2026, /2027, etc.

## v1 Scope (Locked)

| Item                        | Decision                                      |
|-----------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Logical grid                | 1,500 × 1,500 = 2,250,000 pixels             |
| Display                     | Fully responsive, fits any screen, zoom + pan |
| Minimum permanent block     | 10 × 10 pixels                                |
| Free Pioneer slots          | 100 × 10×10 (tweet-to-claim)                  |
| Base price                  | $1 per pixel                                  |
| Early pricing               | Tiered discounts that phase up to $1          |
| The Crown                   | Yes – central outbid zone with history        |
| Rentals                     | No                                            |
| Multi-year support          | Designed for, only 2026 live in v1            |
| Secondary marketplace       | No (future)                                   |

## Pricing Structure (v1)

**Permanent plots**
- 100 free Pioneer 10×10 slots (tweet required)
- Then staged discounts:
  - First ~5% of board: $0.50/px
  - Next tier: $0.75/px
  - Next: $0.90/px
  - Thereafter: $1.00/px
- Larger blocks may receive small bulk incentives later if needed

**The Crown**
- Starts at a low seed bid (or platform-seeded)
- Anyone can take it by paying at least $1 more than the current bid
- Platform keeps 100% of outbid revenue
- Full public history of previous holders

## Key User Benefits

- Own a permanent piece of the 2026 builder landscape
- Real dofollow-style backlink (or strong visible link)
- Public Builder Card with story, tags, and links
- Shareable claim moment
- Being part of a collective, visual record of who was shipping in 2026
- Possibility of holding The Crown (temporary glory + history entry)

## Non-Goals for v1

- Complex heat / spillover pricing across the whole grid
- Rentals or landlord mechanics
- NFT minting
- Secondary resale marketplace
- Multi-year boards live simultaneously
- Heavy social features (comments, follows, etc.)

## Success Metrics (early)

- Pioneer slots claimed + associated tweets
- % of board filled in first 30 / 90 days
- Number of unique Builder Cards
- Organic shares and screenshots
- Crown takeover frequency and total outbid revenue
- Qualitative: does it feel fun and worth talking about?
